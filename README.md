#Cross Industry Capital Allocation and Revenue Leak Monitor

📊 Project Overview

Cross Industry Capital Allocation and Revenue Leak Monitor is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to provide a consolidated view of revenue performance, customer behavior, product performance, returns, and potential revenue leakage across different business dimensions.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into actionable insights through interactive KPIs, slicers, and analytical visualizations. It enables decision-makers to monitor financial performance, understand customer activity and churn, identify product-level opportunities, and detect areas where revenue may be lost.

The project is structured into three analytical pages, each focusing on a different area of business performance.

---

🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Monitor overall revenue and order performance.
- Analyze revenue across countries, categories, devices, and payment methods.
- Track customer acquisition, activity, churn, and spending behavior.
- Identify high-value and low-performing customer segments.
- Evaluate product performance using revenue and ratings.
- Monitor product returns and identify categories/products with high return rates.
- Analyze delivery performance and discount impact.
- Provide management with a centralized view for data-driven decision-making.
